# One-Line Office Joke: 24-Hour Sprint

A one-line office joke entry for the make.fun bounty "One-Line Office Joke: 24-Hour Sprint". The program `main.py` writes a single joke to `/output/entry.txt` — a meeting scheduled to discuss why we have too many meetings, followed by a follow-up booked just to recap the first one.

> Our manager scheduled a meeting to discuss why we have too many meetings, then booked a follow-up to recap the first one.

Run it with `python3 main.py`. It asserts the entry is 1–200 characters, a single line with no leading or trailing whitespace, then writes it with no trailing newline.
